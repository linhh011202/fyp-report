\chapter{System Architecture}
\label{chap:architecture}

This chapter presents the Gateway Dashboard system architecture at a high level, focusing on how the key components interact and the design decisions that enable the platform to scale and evolve. Rather than diving into implementation details, we examine the technology choices, communication patterns, and data management approach that professors and stakeholders need to understand the system's overall design.
